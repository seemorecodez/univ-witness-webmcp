# SeemoreCodez Project Policy

This project follows the SeemoreCodez founder-led MIT open-core policy.

## Policy

- Public source code is MIT licensed.
- SeemoreCodez is the default copyright holder.
- The MIT license is not modified with custom restrictions or contributor upside terms.
- Founder authority, contributor terms, trademark use, and commercial terms are documented separately.
- Open-source contribution does not create ownership, governance, compensation, equity, revenue-share, or sale-proceeds rights.
- Any contributor upside requires a separate signed written agreement.

## Commercial Rights

SeemoreCodez may sell official support, implementation, institutional onboarding, private packs, hosted infrastructure, custom integrations, training, certification, security review support, and related services.

## Contributor Upside

The project may operate a separate contributor upside program. It is not part of the MIT license and is not automatic. Eligibility, measurement, selection, legal review, and payment mechanics must be handled in a separate written agreement.
